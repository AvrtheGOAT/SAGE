Terms and conditions

Last updated 11 November 2026

These terms cover your use of the SAGE wristband, voice assistant and family dashboard. By creating a family account or using SAGE, you agree to them.

1. What SAGE is, and is not

SAGE is a support tool that helps families keep in touch with an elder’s everyday wellbeing. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat any condition, and is not a replacement for emergency services. Heart rate, SpO2 and routine changes are trends, not medical readings. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in India) directly.

2. Alerts depend on things outside our control

Fall detection, SOS and reminders need the band to be charged, worn and connected, and they need working Wi-Fi, mobile networks and phones. SAGE can miss a fall or deliver an alert late. Please keep other ways of checking in, and keep emergency contacts up to date.

3. Your account

  • Keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens under your family account.
  • Only add family members you trust to see health information.
  • Use SAGE only for a person who knows about it and agrees to wear the band, or whom you are legally allowed to care for.

4. Fair use

Don’t misuse SAGE: no attempts to break its security, access other families’ data, or send false SOS alerts on purpose. We may suspend accounts that put others at risk.

5. Your data

Your family’s information belongs to you. We handle it as described in our privacy policy.

6. Liability

We work hard to keep SAGE reliable, but provide it “as is”. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for harm that results from a missed or delayed alert, a wrong reading, or an outage. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under consumer protection law.
