What SAGE can do

Quiet support, when it matters.

Useful signals and simple ways to stay connected—without pretending to replace a doctor.

Fall detection

The band looks for impact followed by stillness and sounds a siren on the band.

SOS, with a safety net

One press starts a 5-second cancel window, then alerts family. If nobody responds, it escalates to a backup contact, then emergency services. Family decides first.

Everyday health trends

Live heart rate and blood oxygen (SpO2) from the band, for trends and alerts—not diagnosis.

Hindi + English voice help

A bilingual voice assistant offers medicine reminders and a friendly morning check-in.

Changes from their usual

Notices routine changes, like missed reminders or slower replies, and suggests mentioning them to a doctor. It never diagnoses dementia.

Plain words, past weeks

An AI care agent explains changes to family in plain language and remembers previous weeks. One account can look after several elders.
